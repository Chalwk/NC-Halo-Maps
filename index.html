About the Cartographer

NakedChick has been mapping for the Halo PC/CE community for years, crafting original multiplayer layouts for CTF, Slayer, Oddball, and Race. Every release on this site ships as a ready-to-install map pack - grab it, drop it in your Halo maps folder, and you're set.
